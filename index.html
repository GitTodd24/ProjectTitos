About Titos

Welcome to Titos Sports Bar and Grill, Johns Creek's favorite spot for food, drinks, and sports! Whether you're here to catch the big game, enjoy a night out with friends, or simply grab a delicious meal, we've got you covered.

Our menu features mouth-watering wings, juicy burgers, fresh salads, and so much more. With multiple large-screen TVs throughout our venue, you'll never miss a moment of the action. Come experience the perfect blend of great atmosphere, outstanding service, and unbeatable food!
